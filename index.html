01. Executive Summary

Amora Ventures specializes in the high-efficiency production of 98.5% Industrial Grade Sulfuric Acid. Utilizing a 4-pass catalytic conversion and Double Contact Double Absorption (DCDA) technology, our facility is engineered to exceed environmental standards while maintaining peak profitability. Our 12 A team focused on Circular Energy Recovery, ensuring all exothermic heat is converted into high-value utility steam.

02. Operational Flowchart

Phase I: Sulfur Liquefaction

Raw elemental sulfur is heated to 145°C for optimal atomization.

↓

Phase II: Thermal Oxidation

Combustion in the furnace creates $SO_2$ at 1,050°C.

↓

Phase III: Catalytic Conversion

Oxidation to $SO_3$ via Vanadium Pentoxide beds.

↓

Phase IV: Primary Absorption

Inter-stage absorption maximizes the chemical equilibrium shift.

↓

Phase V: Final Polishing

Residual gases are processed to ensure near-zero $SO_x$ emissions.

06. Reaction Kinetics

Reaction Rate ($r$) = $k \cdot [SO_2] \cdot [O_2]^{0.5} \cdot [SO_3]^{-1}$
